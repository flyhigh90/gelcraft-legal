Privacy Policy — GelCraft

Last updated: 11 August 2026

GelCraft is a bioprinting and cell-culture laboratory assistant for researchers. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

Contact for privacy questions: privacy@gelcraft.app

Summary

Your laboratory work stays on your device. The only information sent to our servers is the minimum needed to run your account: your email address, your display name and the lab name you choose to enter.

GelCraft contains no advertising, no ad identifiers, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party marketing services. We never sell or share your personal information.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information (sent to our servers)

When you create an account or sign in with Google, we store:

DataWhy
Email addressIdentifies your account and lets you sign in
Display nameShown in the app; identifies you in your lab
Lab / organisation nameOptional; labels your records
Account created / last active timestampsLets us see whether accounts are in active use
Device model and Android versionDiagnosing device-specific defects

This information is held in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). No other category of personal data is transmitted off your device.

1.2 Laboratory data (stays on your device)

Everything you produce in the app is stored only in local storage on your device and is never uploaded to us:

  • Bioink and hydrogel formulations, calculations and print parameters
  • Experiments, batch logs, protocols and notebook entries
  • Reagent and stock records
  • Sketches, G-code and 3D models you generate
  • Photos you attach or annotate

We cannot see this data. If you uninstall the app or delete your account, it is gone unless you exported it yourself.

1.3 Camera

The camera is used only when you open the QR/barcode scanner to identify a reagent. Frames are analysed on your device and are never recorded, stored or transmitted. You may decline the camera permission; only the scanner stops working.

1.4 Bioprinter connection

If you connect to a bioprinter, the app opens a direct connection to that printer's address on your own local network to read live telemetry. This traffic goes only between your device and your printer. It does not reach our servers.

1.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect location data, contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, health records about you, or financial information.

2. How we use information

Account information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in;
  • Display your profile inside the app;
  • Maintain an internal count of active accounts;
  • Investigate faults you report.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making, and we do not sell it.

3. Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) processes and stores account data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose that sign-in method.
  • Where we are legally required to do so by a valid legal process.

4. Retention

Account data is retained while your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed from our systems as described below. On-device laboratory data is retained until you delete it, delete your account, or uninstall the app.

5. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your cloud profile, your sign-in identity, and all laboratory data stored on that device. It cannot be undone — export anything you want to keep first.

By email: write to privacy@gelcraft.app from your account's email address and we will delete the account and its data within 30 days.

6. Security

  • All traffic to our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Passwords for in-app accounts are never stored in readable form; they are hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations) with a per-account random salt.
  • Credential stores are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Access controls on our database restrict every account to its own record.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using measures appropriate to its sensitivity.

7. Children

GelCraft is a professional research tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object to it or lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Exercise any of these by writing to privacy@gelcraft.app. Access, correction and deletion are also available directly in Settings.

Our legal basis for processing account information under the GDPR is performance of a contract (providing the account you asked for) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service working.

9. International transfers

Data is processed on Google Cloud infrastructure and may be handled in countries other than yours, including the United States, under Google's contractual data-protection commitments.

10. Research-use disclaimer

GelCraft supports laboratory research. It is not a medical device, and its calculations, protocols and print parameters are not clinical or diagnostic advice. Always validate outputs against your own laboratory's validated procedures.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially we will update the date above and notify you in the app before the change takes effect.
