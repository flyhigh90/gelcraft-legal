Delete your GelCraft account

You can permanently delete your GelCraft account and everything stored under it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open GelCraft and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Application section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Read the confirmation, then tap Delete permanently.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You are signed out as soon as it completes.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

Email privacy@gelcraft.app from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Delete my account".

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may ask you to verify ownership of the address before proceeding.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes, permanently:

  • Your account profile — name, email address and lab name
  • Your sign-in identity, including Google Sign-In linkage
  • Your record on our servers
  • All laboratory data held on the device: bioink formulations, experiments, batch logs, protocols, notebook entries, reagent and stock records, sketches, generated G-code and 3D models, and any photos you attached

This cannot be undone. Most of your laboratory data exists only on your device — we hold no copy of it and cannot restore it for you. If you want to keep any of your work, export it from within the app before deleting your account.

What we retain, and for how long

Nothing. We keep no residual copy of your account or your laboratory data after deletion. We retain no backup of your records beyond the point of deletion, except where a specific legal obligation requires it — in which case the data is retained only for as long as that obligation lasts, and is not used for any other purpose.
